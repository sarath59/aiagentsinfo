## What Klarna’s filing actually prints

Use the Form 20-F, not the February 2024 launch post, if you quote Klarna in 2026. The filing says the assistant launched in February 2024 with OpenAI, and that it manages customer service, refunds, and returns. For the year ended December 31, 2025 it says the assistant handled 80 percent of customer-service chats, according to Klarna’s service chat logs, with no drop in satisfaction on internal surveys.

The same document prints two equivalent-headcount estimates, and they are not the same sentence.

- One passage says the assistant has had 31 million conversations since launch, resolved queries in two minutes versus 12 minutes for human agents as of September 2024, did the equivalent work of over 700 full-time agents based on the 2024 reduction in conversations those agents handled, and delivered $39 million in cost savings in 2024.
- A later passage says the 2025 work was the equivalent of over 850 full-time agents, estimated from the average monthly reduction in chat and telephone conversations handled by full-time agents in 2025, and about $59 million in cost savings in 2025.

Both figures are Klarna’s estimates. The methods are not the same year. Do not add 700 and 850, and do not treat $59 million as audited savings. The risk factor still calls the system a customer-service chatbot, and it warns that generative output can be wrong. The same filing says Klarna does not use generative AI for credit underwriting. The assistant is a support and shopping workflow. It is not the credit model.

The US voice line is a second product. ElevenLabs’ February 11, 2026 post says Klarna launched it as the first line of phone support for US customers, that it escalates, and that resolutions are up to 10 times faster for queries the agent handles. “Up to 10 times” is ElevenLabs’ phrase. It is not in the 20-F passages above.

## What makes it agentic

A system is agentic on this page when it does five things.

1. **Plan.** The model chooses a next step from a goal, not from a script that already named every step.
2. **Tool calling.** It requests a tool: a shell, a file edit, a browser action, a refund, a search, a cart, or a security API.
3. **Act.** The harness runs that tool on something outside the chat window.
4. **Loop.** The result comes back, and the model can take another step.
5. **Guardrails.** A permission check, a spend cap, a plan you can edit, or a person who must approve before the action counts.

A chatbot fails 3 and 4. It replies. A fixed automation can act, and it can even loop, but a person wrote the path. The model did not pick the tool. That distinction is the long version of [how AI agents work](/blog/how-do-ai-agents-work). The protocol many of these tools now speak is [MCP](/mcp). The short definition is the [Model Context Protocol](/glossary/model-context-protocol) glossary entry.

One sentence on why each row qualifies:

- **Claude Code** qualifies because the harness runs the tool the model just chose, then feeds the result back.
- **Codex** qualifies because a cloud task keeps editing and reviewing after you close the laptop, and the handoff is a pull request rather than a paragraph.
- **Devin** qualifies because a session can use a shell, an editor, and a browser, and you can take the editor back mid-run.
- **Copilot’s cloud agent** qualifies because it plans, edits, and runs tests in a sandbox, then waits on your review unless you asked for the pull request up front.
- **Cursor Cloud Agents** qualify because the VM builds and tests the change, can drive a desktop, and can call MCP servers before the branch is pushed.
- **Klarna’s assistant** qualifies because the filing says it completes service tasks, including refunds and returns, rather than only drafting an answer.
- **Klarna’s US voice agent** qualifies because it holds the phone call, gathers context, and escalates, which is a loop with a handoff.
- **Crocs’ Rivet** qualifies because an orchestrator routes work across shopper and service agents, including returns and conversational checkout. Orchestration is the [multi-agent](/blog/what-is-multi-agent-orchestration) pattern, and the glossary term is [orchestration](/glossary/orchestration).
- **Nestlé’s agent** qualifies because the case study describes autonomous resolution of a consumer question, with a handoff when a person is required.
- **Southwest’s assistant** qualifies because it takes the first pass on a traveler’s request and pulls in a rep only when the request needs one.
- **Trustpilot’s Sam** qualifies because it authenticates, answers, and routes. Authentication is a tool call, not a paragraph.
- **L’Oréal’s deployment** is the edge case. It calls tools that draft and that generate a promo code, but an advisor still owns the send. Treat it as agentic assistance, not an unattended agent.
- **Google’s Canada checkout** qualifies because the shopping flow can reach payment for a named retailer, which is an action, not a link list.
- **Copilot Checkout** qualifies because Microsoft’s page says the purchase can finish inside Copilot, on the merchant’s systems.
- **Alexa auto-buy** qualifies because Amazon says it can place the order when a price is reached, without a fresh click on that order.
- **ChatGPT Wallet** qualifies as a guarded agent. The dot can pay, and the payment does not happen until you approve that purchase.
- **ChatGPT desktop Computer Use** qualifies because the model asks for a click or a key and the app runs it. [Computer use](/glossary/computer-use) is that loop.
- **Claude computer use** qualifies for the same reason, inside the plans the help page names, and not on Team or Enterprise as of November 9, 2026.
- **Chrome auto browse** qualifies because the browser takes the steps. It is [browser use](/glossary/browser-use), the narrower case of computer use.
- **Deep research** qualifies because it writes a plan, searches, reads tools, and revises until the report is done. It does not qualify as a system that writes back to your apps.
- **Attack Investigation Agent** qualifies because it runs a multi-step investigation from one alert and produces a verdict you are told to review before you act on it.
- **AI Front Door** qualifies, on the company’s release, because the voice agent carries an inbound clinical-access call and is deployed with named health systems. The release is not a peer-reviewed outcome study.

## What this means for you

**Builders.** Pick the harness before you pick the demo. Claude Code, Codex, Devin, Copilot, and Cursor Cloud Agents are five different places the loop can run, and they do not share a stop rule. Copilot’s Actions-based session ends at 59 minutes. Cursor’s cloud agent can call MCP and can also run hooks you put in the repo. If you cannot name the tool list, the approval gate, and the eval that fails a bad run, you have a chatbot with extra buttons. The comparison set is [Best AI agent harnesses](/best/ai-agent-harnesses). Cost per task is not the seat price. Token price, retries, and tool JSON sit outside the monthly sticker. The [12-month calendar](/reports/ai-agents-next-12-months) dates the token steps. It does not convert them into a successful task.

**Buyers.** Ask which row you are buying. A support agent that deflects 28 percent of cases, vendor-reported, is not a coding agent that opens a pull request, and it is not a checkout agent that can spend. Ask for the human stop in one sentence: who approves a refund, a payment, a pull request, or a security remediation. Then ask for the source of the percentage. Klarna’s 80 percent is a filing plus chat logs. Southwest’s 45 percent is a Salesforce case study. Those are different kinds of evidence. If the seller cannot point at a page and a date, treat the number as a slide.

**Merchants.** The shopping rows do not behave the same way. Google named Canadian retailers on November 4, 2026. Microsoft’s page, re-read November 14, limits Copilot Checkout to a US dollar sale. Amazon can auto-buy at a price. ChatGPT’s ordinary shopping flow still sends the buyer to the store, and Wallet is a separate approval. The month-by-month record is [AI shopping agents, the next 6 months](/reports/ai-shopping-agents-next-6-months). The directory of products is the [AI agent directory](/directory).

## Where these systems fail, on the record

The interesting deployments are also the ones with a public limit.

OpenAI’s help page, read November 9, 2026, says ChatGPT agent is no longer available and points people to ChatGPT Work and the cloud browser. The same page says Operator was folded into agent mode and that the Operator website is no longer accessible. A 2025 list that still leads with Operator is describing a product OpenAI has taken down.

[OSWorld 2.0](https://osworld-v2.xlang.ai/), read November 9, 2026, prints a 20.6 percent binary completion for Claude Opus 4.8 at 500 steps on 108 long tasks, and a 54.8 percent partial score. Above 163 minutes, binary completion on that plot falls to zero for every model the page keeps. Computer use is a real product. It is not a solved one. The product calendar is [browser and computer-use agents](/reports/browser-and-computer-use-agents-next-6-months).

On November 11, 2026, United Airlines said it is barring unauthorized third-party personal AI agents from booking, ticketing, and distribution. A shopping agent that can click a store is not a shopping agent that can buy a seat.

GitHub’s own docs say Copilot can produce incorrect code, including code with security vulnerabilities, and that session logs do not replace your review. Microsoft’s Attack Investigation Agent page says the report is AI-generated and that you review it before remediation. Deep research does not get write actions on connected apps. Those are the guardrails. They are also the admission that the loop is not the last word.

Klarna’s filing is the cleanest customer-service source in this set, and it still warns that generative tools can be inaccurate, biased, or the wrong fit for the job. An 80 percent chat share is not a claim that the other 20 percent, or the credit decision, should be automated.

They are not a named company running the loop on its own customers, so they are not rows. Abridge’s 2026 clinical assistant stays off the table because the clinician directs the query. ServiceNow’s assist meter is a price, not a customer. If a later primary page names the customer, the action, and the date, it can be added. Until then the count stays 22.

### What is an example of agentic AI?

Claude Code is one. Anthropic’s agent-loop docs, read November 8, 2026, describe a model that proposes a step, a harness that runs an allowed tool, and a loop that continues until the model stops, an error hits, or a person must approve. Klarna’s assistant is another. The Form 20-F says it handles customer-service chats, including refunds and returns, and that it handled 80 percent of those chats in 2025 according to Klarna’s own chat logs.

### Is ChatGPT agentic AI?

A normal ChatGPT reply is generative. It writes text and stops. Deep research, desktop Computer Use, and ChatGPT Wallet are agentic modes: they plan, call tools, and act, and a person can edit the plan, take the screen back, or approve the payment. OpenAI’s help page, read November 9, 2026, says the older ChatGPT agent product is no longer available and that the Operator website is no longer accessible.

### What is agentic AI vs generative AI, with examples?

Generative AI produces an output and stops. A chat answer is generative. Agentic AI adds a loop: the model chooses a next action, a harness runs a tool, and the result comes back. Claude Code editing a repository is agentic. A chat reply that only suggests a code change is generative. Anthropic’s November 2, 2026 prospectus draws that line, and [how AI agents work](/blog/how-do-ai-agents-work) walks through it.

### What are examples of agentic AI in customer service?

Klarna’s assistant, Crocs’ Rivet, Nestlé’s consumer agent, Southwest’s help assistant, and Trustpilot’s Sam. Klarna’s 80 percent is from a filing and chat logs. The Salesforce percentages are vendor-reported. L’Oréal’s deployment drafts for advisors, and a person still owns the case.

### What are examples of agentic AI in healthcare?

Hippocratic AI’s April 16, 2026 release says AI Front Door is being deployed with Wellspan and Cincinnati Children’s as initial partners. It describes a voice agent for inbound access and does not print a resolution rate. A clinician-directed chart assistant is a different product, and this page does not promote it to the same row.

### What are examples of agentic AI in finance?

Klarna is the example with a filing. The 20-F reports the 80 percent chat share and also says Klarna does not use generative AI for credit underwriting. Google, Microsoft, Amazon, and ChatGPT Wallet are the payments examples, and each one stops at a different approval rule. The commerce term is [agentic commerce](/glossary/agentic-commerce).

### What makes an AI system agentic instead of a chatbot?

It plans, it calls a tool, it acts, it loops, and a guardrail or a person can stop it. A chatbot stops at the text. A fixed workflow can act, but the path was written before the run. The [guardrail](/glossary/guardrail) and the [human in the loop](/glossary/human-in-the-loop) are the stop, not a footnote.

### Do these agents run without a person?

No row on this page is an unattended company. Coding agents hand you a branch or an editor. Wallet waits for a biometric approval. The security agent’s page tells you to review the report before you remediate. Deep research reads. It does not write to your apps.
