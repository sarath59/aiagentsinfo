> **Sample article.** This is a format demo for the “This Week in Agents” strip. Do not treat the bullets as news. When you replace this file, every item should have a primary source in the list below.

## How this strip is supposed to work

Each week, three to seven items. Each item: one sentence what happened, one sentence why it matters for agents, one link. No recap of social media rumors. No user-count theater.

## Sample slots (replace)

- **Models and labs.** Slot for a real lab post or paper. Point to the PDF or changelog, not a screenshot of a thread.
- **Tools and SaaS.** Slot for a real product note. Add or update the [directory](/directory) listing the same week.
- **Agents in the world.** Slot for shopping, education, or task-performance reporting with a named source.
- **Harnesses and evals.** Slot for a real benchmark refresh or harness write-up. Cross-link [research](/research) when there is a paper.

## What we will not do

We will not invent launches, download counts, or “industry firsts.” If a claim cannot be sourced, it does not ship. See [about](/about) for editorial rules.
