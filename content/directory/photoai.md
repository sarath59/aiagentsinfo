Photo AI, also searched as photoai and PhotoAI, trains a likeness and then spends credits on photos and short videos of that person. The pricing page links a FAQ titled "Who created Photo AI? Meet Pieter Levels, the Founder," which is the site's own credit to Pieter Levels, also known as levelsio. This listing uses that link title. It does not add a biography.

The job is narrow. You upload photos of one person, the product trains a model of them, and later prompts spend credits on headshots, outfits, dating photos, and, on the higher plans, video. People searching photo ai or photoai pricing are usually trying to learn whether custom prompts, commercial use, and video are on the cheap plan. On November 14, 2026 they were not.

## Pricing

[photoai.com/pricing](https://photoai.com/pricing) was fetched twice on 2026-11-14. Both responses were 110,765 bytes and contained the same plan strings.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $19 | $9 | $99 | 50 |
| Pro | $49 | $29 | $349 | 1,000 |
| Max | $99 | $49 | $599 | 3,000 |
| Ultra | $199 | $99 | $1,199 | 10,000 |

The page says yearly saves up to 57%. The yearly Starter card says 6+ months free and "save $129" against paying $19 every month. Pro says save $239. Max says save $589. Ultra says save $1,189. Those savings are the page's arithmetic against the monthly card, not a coupon this desk tested.

What the monthly cards say you can make with the credit pool: Starter, 5 Nano Banana 2 photos or 10 Seedream 5.5 photos. Pro, 100 or 200 of those. Max, 300 Nano Banana 2 photos, or 600 Seedream 5.5 photos, or 16 Seedance 2.5 videos at 15 seconds. Ultra, 1,000 or 2,000 of those photos, or 40 of those videos. Those are alternative uses of the same credits, not a stack of all three.

Starter is marked low photo quality and low likeness, one photo at a time, one AI model a month. Pro is medium, four photos at once, three models a month. Max is high, eight at once, ten models a month. Ultra is the top likeness label, sixteen at once, fifty models a month. Every plan says 48 free photos for each model you create. The cards multiply that out: 48 total on Starter, 144 on Pro, 480 on Max, 2,400 on Ultra, because the model allowance grows.

## What is locked until a higher plan

The comparison on the same page is the part worth reading before you pay $19. Starter does not include writing your own prompts, remixing a photo, importing photos, a commercial license, AI video, edit and crop and zoom-out, a magic upscaler, try-on for Shopify, early access, unlimited storage, or faster support. Pro turns on custom prompts, remix, import, and still withholds commercial use and video. Max is where commercial use, video, editing, the upscaler, Shopify try-on, and early access turn on. Ultra adds unlimited photo storage and priority response times. If you need a photo you can put in an ad, the card that says yes is Max at $99 a month, not Starter.

There is no $0 plan on this pricing page. The "48 free photos" line is included output after you train a model on a paid plan. A separate FAQ link mentions coupons. This desk did not treat a coupon as the price.

## Best for

Someone who wants photos of a real person, in a consistent likeness, and who has compared the credit examples on the card with the number of shots they actually need.

## Watch out for

Starter cannot take your prompt. Commercial rights and video start at Max. Yearly is a prepaid bill ($99, $349, $599, or $1,199), not a month you can drop. The photo counts and the video counts are alternate ways to spend one credit pool. A Seedance clip on Max is one of 16 in the example, which makes video the expensive path. Training a model of a person who did not agree to it is your problem. The site does not review that photo for you.

## Alternatives

[HeadshotPro](/directory/headshotpro) is the other likeness-headshot listing. [Promptwise](/directory/promptwise) sells a pool of image and video models without training a private face first, from $19 a month. [Midjourney](/directory/midjourney) is a general image generator. [Kolbo.AI](/directory/kolbo) keeps many of the same model names in one workspace, with its own credit rules. [Speel](/directory/speel) is built for product ads and avatars, not a trained photo of you. Pick PhotoAI when the subject is a specific person and the plan card's license line matches how you will use the file.
