invideo AI is a video editor that also spends credits on generative models. Starter, at $20 a seat a month, includes 400 credits, the timeline, and multiplayer editing. The annual card is $10 a seat. Plus, at $60 or $30 annually, is the plan the page uses for Seedance 2.5.

## Best for

A team that wants a shared editor and a credit pool for model clips, and that can live with the credit table changing.

## Watch out for

Credits do not roll over. The FAQ says model prices can be updated with no notice, so a 400-credit month is not a fixed number of clips. The page's own example puts a 5-second Seedance 2.5 clip at about 56 credits in 720p, which is roughly seven of those clips on Starter if you spend the month on nothing else. Seedance 2.5 is a Plus feature. The cards show 50% off on annual billing, and a banner says a further discount appears after signup, so the number on the card is not a promise of the checkout price. Seats do not share one person's credits with a guest in a way that raises the seat's own 400.
