Superwhisper turns speech into text on Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android. The free app stays on the device with local Whisper models. Pro is what adds the AI layer: cloud voice models, modes that rewrite what you said, meeting notes, and transcription of files you already have. There is no separate license per computer. One Pro key covers the devices listed on the plans page.
