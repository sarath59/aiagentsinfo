Clip Utils, also searched as cliputils and clip utils, is a set of browser tools from Ojas Sharma for video and audio. The site is cliputils.com. The AI pieces are subtitles, transcription, translation, dubbing, and voice-over. Most of the processing stays on your machine, so you are not uploading the file to a render queue. The free tier needs no account. A paid pass removes the short-clip limits.

## Best for

Someone who needs subtitles, a translation, or a dubbed track on one video and does not want a subscription studio.

## Watch out for

The price depends on the country. The headline many visitors see is $1.49, $3.99, and $24.99. The US table on the same page is $2.99, $5.99, and $29.99. Monthly and yearly plans renew until you cancel. The 7-day pass does not. Free text-to-video clips carry a small tag. Free translation stops at the first 8 minutes.
