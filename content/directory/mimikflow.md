MimikFlow, from Mo Alani, is a LinkedIn prospecting agent. It watches signals such as a new job, a hiring post, or a funding round, then writes a first message for each person. On Discovery you still answer the replies. On Pro the agent answers and books the meeting, and the page lists MCP so ChatGPT or Claude can sit on the same workflow.

## Best for

A founder who sells on LinkedIn and wants the research and the first note done, with the option to let the agent book the call.

## Watch out for

Discovery does not talk to the prospect after the opening sequence. That is the Pro plan, at €97/month. Prices are euros and already include VAT. LinkedIn itself caps a free account at 100 invitations a week, which is below the Pro plan's 200. WhatsApp is a separate +€19 line, and the trial lets you test only one channel. The pricing page is in French.
