Onton is a shopping search with a room attached. You look for real products, then generate images of them in a space: Imagine, Remix, Furnish, and, on higher plans, Edit. Free is $0 for 10 images and 10 canvases, and it does not ask for a card. Basic, at $8 a month billed annually, is where Furnish is included.

## Best for

A shopper who is furnishing a room and wants the product search and the picture of the room in the same place.

## Watch out for

The meter is images, not items purchased. Free stops at 10 images and does not include Furnish. Every paid card says billed annually. The page does not print what a single month costs if you refuse the annual term. Edit, the step that changes an image you already have, starts at Plus ($20 a month billed annually). Studio's extra line is a CEO call, not more shopping coverage. The boost is the credit type the page says never expires. The FAQ asks whether unused monthly images roll over, and the answers were not in the page HTML read November 12, so that rule is not stated here.
