BeamTake finds moments in a long recording, reframes them vertical, and burns in word-by-word captions. The meter is the source file. A 40-minute podcast costs 40 minutes whether you export 3 clips or 20, and a re-render of that file does not cost the minutes again. Creator is the plan that removes the watermark. The monthly price on the comparison table is $19. The yearly card shows $12 a month because $144 is billed for the year.

## Best for

A YouTuber or a podcaster who will upload the same long file once and pull many captioned clips from it.

## Watch out for

Free projects are deleted after 5 days. Free scheduling is TikTok only, and the watermark stays. Unused minutes reset at the start of the month. They do not roll. Studio's yearly discount is printed as 30% off, Creator's as 37% off, so the two plans are not the same saving. The page does not publish a Stripe revenue figure. A TrustMRR profile for this product is verified with a Creem API key, which is not a Stripe figure, so no revenue is quoted here.
