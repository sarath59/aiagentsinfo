Speel, at speel.co, makes product images and short videos that are meant to look like creator posts: an avatar, lip sync, a product in someone's hands, an outfit swap. It is a creative app. It is not an agent framework. Searches for speel and speel.co are this product. The old pricing URL, speel.co/pricing, returned HTTP 404 on November 14, 2026. Starter's $49 and $35 appear in both places. Growth's $109 and $79 appear in both places. Pro does not. The card says $139 a month. The FAQ says $179 a month. Both say the yearly Pro equivalent is $129. TrustMRR's pricing blurb on November 14, 2026 says Starter $49, Growth $109, Pro $139, which matches the cards and not the FAQ. Quote the conflict. Do not pick one number and drop the other.

The FAQ also says all plans include unlimited workspace members and every model and feature. It says yearly saves about 29%, while the toggle label says 30% off. Credit cost depends on the model, the resolution, and the duration, and the FAQ says the cost is shown on the Generate button before you run. Advanced models spend more of the pool than the "up to" examples.

## The free trial

The FAQ says new users can try Speel with 90 credits. One answer says that makes 1 video and up to 5 images. Another line says about 3 videos and 6 images. A third says the trial is over 24 hours. A later answer says the trial is not time-limited and the credits last until they are used. There is no auto-renewal. You are billed only if you upgrade. The 90-credit trial is one-time and for new users. Those sentences are all on the homepage. They do not agree about the clock or the video count. The part they agree on is 90 credits, no automatic charge.

Commercial use is a clear yes in the FAQ. You own the creatives. The page says there is no licensing fee and no attribution requirement for ads, social posts, and landing-page visuals. Features named on the cards include 35+ languages, lip sync, a realistic avatar, a skin enhancer, product holding, outfit swapping, magic edit, a mobile app, and UGC Studio. The monthly cards cap length at 1 minute. A yearly Starter card says up to 1 minute as well.

## Best for

A brand that needs short UGC-style ads of a product being held or worn, and that will check the Generate button for the credit cost of the model it actually picked.

## Watch out for

Do not quote Pro at $179 and $139 as if they were two plans. They are two texts on one page, checked twice on November 14, 2026. The video caps on the cards (55, then 660 on a cheaper card) are not the same unit as the FAQ's 1,200 credits. speel.co/pricing is a 404, so the homepage is the source until they publish a single table. The trial's 90 credits are not a free month. Yearly is billed annually at the lower per-month card.
