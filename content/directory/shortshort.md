shortshort takes one long video and cuts captioned vertical shorts that end on a full sentence. The crop follows the face. The homepage accepts MP4, MOV, or WebM up to 2 GB, or a YouTube link, and it can schedule the shorts to YouTube, with TikTok in early access. You pay for the minutes of the source file. Asking for more shorts from that file does not add credits. Editing and exporting the finished shorts do not spend credits either. The AI analysis does.

## Best for

Someone with a talk, a podcast, or a course who wants the shorts cut on sentence boundaries and does not want a per-clip fee.

## Watch out for

The free plan publishes each exported short, with the source video and the transcript, in a public community gallery. Starter is the plan that can keep exports out of that gallery, and it is the plan that removes the watermark. Prices on the page are euros before VAT. A US card is not billed the dollar figure in the footnote. That figure is the page's own conversion at the 16 October 2026 ECB rate. Monthly credits on Starter roll over.
