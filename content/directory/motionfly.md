Motionfly, also searched as motionfly ai, motion fly, and motionfly pricing, makes product videos from a prompt. The homepage, read 2026-11-14, says it generates product-launch videos, SaaS explainers, and app-launch videos with 3D phone and laptop mockups, text and logo animation, voiceover, and sound. The formats it names on the use-case pages are a Product Hunt gallery video, an App Store preview, a sales demo, and a 3D mockup pass. People looking for a motion fly alternative are usually comparing this with a talking-head tool or a timeline editor. Motionfly's own FAQ says it does not put a founder on camera.

TrustMRR's founder field, checked 2026-11-14, reads NATE G, United States, founded April 2026. That is the public profile on TrustMRR, not a bio on motionfly.co/about that this desk retyped.

## How a video gets made

The homepage steps are short. You describe the product. Motionfly builds the script, the 3D devices, the type, the voice, and the sound. A second path is the desktop app: record the product and it returns a showcase with auto-zoom and 3D mockups. The FAQ says most videos are generated in under 60 seconds, and that you do not get a timeline, keyframes, or a render queue. The use-case pages say one render can be exported at 16:9, 9:16, and 1:1. App Store previews are described as 9:16, 15 to 30 seconds, without third-party UI in the frame. Product Hunt videos are described around 45 to 60 seconds, with text that still reads if the viewer has sound off.

A block on the homepage says you can ask Claude or ChatGPT for the video. The three steps there are: open Motionfly so it connects itself, ask for the video and share the site, then approve the scenes and take the MP4. The model chip on that demo reads Sonnet 4.6. Your Claude or ChatGPT subscription pays for that model. The MCP page says this does not spend the Motionfly launch-video budget.

## The agent connection

The page at motionfly.co/mcp, read 2026-11-14, is a troubleshooting guide, and it is also the spec. A small bridge ships inside the Motionfly desktop app. Claude Code, Codex or ChatGPT, or the Claude desktop app starts that bridge. You do not install Node. The desktop app runs a local MCP server on 127.0.0.1 while it is open. On each launch it writes a Motionfly server entry into the config of every AI app it finds. The bridge finds the running app, so a port change does not need a hand edit. The prompt they tell you to use spells the product MotionFly, one of the spellings that shows up in search.

Three things have to be true or the tools do not appear. The desktop app is open and you are signed in. The account is on Premium or Studio. Settings, MCP, MCP server is on. On another plan the same screen shows an Upgrade button. The download link on that page is Motionfly for Mac. This desk did not find a Windows desktop build on the MCP page.

## Best for

A founder who needs a Product Hunt or landing-page video of the actual UI on a phone or laptop, and who would rather approve scenes than edit a timeline.

## Watch out for

Premium and Studio are required for MCP, and their prices are not published. The $50 line is a blog sentence. A free start is claimed, and the limit of that free tier is not in the HTML this desk could read. The desktop MCP server runs only while the Mac app is open. There is no talking-head avatar, which the company's own comparison posts list as a limit. The product is new. TrustMRR's domain rating line for the site was 6 on 2026-11-14. That is an Ahrefs figure on TrustMRR, not a quality score.
