ColorCraft, from John Kirby, is a book factory for adults who sell on Amazon KDP. You describe a coloring book, storybook, comic, or puzzle book. It draws the pages and a cover wrap sized to the page count, then exports files you upload to KDP yourself. It is not a children's app. The buyer is the publisher.

## Best for

A self-publisher who wants interior pages and a KDP cover in one place, and who will still check the files before they go live.

## Watch out for

The trial needs a card. Cancel before day 7 or the plan starts. The trial download is watermarked. Extra covers, lifestyle mockups, and the listing generator start at Creator, $32/month. Mini and Starter show yearly prices ($90 and $140). The higher tiers only show the 17% annual label on this pass, so read those cards before you switch the toggle. Printed copies ship to the US.
