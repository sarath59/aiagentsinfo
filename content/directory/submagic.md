Submagic is a short-video editor: captions, B-roll, zooms, silence removal, and Magic Clips from a long video. David Zitoun and Tsi-fei Chan are the founders named in the homepage schema. Prices are per member. Starter, at $19 a month, is the individual plan, and the card says 45 credits cover 15 videos or 5 Magic Clips, each up to 2 minutes.

## Best for

A creator who already has the footage and wants captions, a cut, and a short clip without a timeline editor.

## Watch out for

The starter card and the comparison table do not agree on API minutes. One starter block prints 100 minutes a month. The comparison table prints 10 minutes for Starter, 10 for Pro, and 100 for Business. Credits are the meter that is consistent: 45, 120, and 300 per member on the yearly cards. A Magic Clip spends more of that pool than a captioned upload. The page equates the starter pool to 5 Magic Clips or 15 videos, so the clip feature is the expensive one. Yearly billing is a prepaid member fee ($144, $276, or $492), not a month-to-month discount you can drop after one month.
