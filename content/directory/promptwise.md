Promptwise, styled PromptWise on its own site and searched as promptwise, is one login for a pool of image, video, and audio models. The pricing URL promptwise.com/pricing redirects to /models-and-pricing/. Both fetches on November 14, 2026 landed there and contained the same dollar amounts. You do not buy a separate subscription per lab. The FAQ says every plan includes all 40+ models and every feature, and that plans differ only in monthly credits and parallel generations.

The page says the monthly prices already are 20% off a list price: Starter $24, Plus $49, Pro $99, Max $186, Max 2 $311, Max 3 $436. Yearly is a further cut against that list. The worked example on the page: Starter is $159 a year instead of $288, and yearly saves $69 compared with paying $19 every month. Prices exclude VAT. Larger plans cost less per credit. The page's own comparison with Starter: 29% less per credit on Plus, 35% on Pro, 42% on Max, 43% on the 6,500-credit option, and 46% on the 9,500-credit option.

Every plan, including Starter, lists the same feature set: all image, video, and audio models, including Seedance 2.5; Flows, UGC Factory, Influencer Studio, Wise, and Studio; MCP for Claude, Cursor, or any MCP client; and a commercial license on what you generate. The tool blurbs on the page: Flows is a node canvas that connects prompts, images, videos, and models. UGC Factory makes ads with AI creators. Influencer Studio keeps a character for a campaign. Wise helps plan ideas and write prompts. Studio is the library. MCP is under Account Settings.

The FAQ says every plan starts with a 7-day trial that includes 50 credits. A card is required at sign-up. After 7 days the plan continues at its normal price. You can cancel anytime. Failed generations are refunded automatically. Sora is not in PromptWise. The FAQ says OpenAI shut down the Sora API on 24 September 2026, and names Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, and Seedance 2.5 among 20 other video models.

## Best for

A person who wants to try several video and image models in a week without a contract at each lab, and who can live inside a credit cap.

## Watch out for

The trial needs a card, and it becomes a paid plan on day 8 unless you cancel. Fifty trial credits are not the Starter allowance of 275. VAT is extra. The "2 each" through "8 each" figures sit on the card next to credits. The FAQ says the plans differ by credits and parallel generations, so read that second number as how many generations run at once, and confirm the column header on the page before you rely on it. Yearly is prepaid ($159 and up). Sora is absent even if an old review mentions it. Credits are PromptWise credits. They are not dollars and not another product's credits.

## Alternatives

[Kolbo.AI](/directory/kolbo) is the other multi-model workspace in this directory, with editor plugins and a different credit table. [PhotoAI](/directory/photoai) trains a likeness of one person. Max there is $99 a month before video is included. [Runway](/directory/runway), [Pika](/directory/pika), and [Midjourney](/directory/midjourney) are single-product generators. [Speel](/directory/speel) is aimed at product ads. Use Promptwise when the menu of models matters more than a plugin inside Premiere.
