FrameLiq is a script-to-video studio. You import a screenplay, or you start from an idea, and it storyboards scene by scene before it renders. The homepage names Kling 4, Veo 3.1 Fast, Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, Seedance 2.0, and 10+ models, plus dialogue, music, and lip-sync, and an MP4 at the end. The $19 Creator plan is the first paid plan, and it does not include the render. Media on that plan is billed to your own fal.ai key.

## Best for

A solo filmmaker who already has a script and a fal.ai account, and who wants the storyboard and the shot list in one project before paying a video model.

## Watch out for

Story credits and Media credits are different meters. Free includes 10 Story credits and zero Media credits, so the Story Engine can be tried and a picture or a clip cannot. On Creator Plus, 800 Media credits are about 1.6 minutes of video for the month. A single 5-second 720p Kling 4 take is about 79 of those credits, so the included pool is a handful of shots, not a scene. The Composer shows the cost before you generate. Bought Media credits do not expire. Monthly ones do. TrustMRR verifies revenue with a Stripe API key.
