Glossa translates a live service while it is happening. A phone on the stage mic sends speech out. Listeners open a page, pick a language, and hear or read the translation. Captions can drop into ProPresenter, OBS, or vMix. The product is aimed at churches and Christian events. The pricing page is in US dollars.

## Best for

A church that already streams, wants spoken translation in the room, and can count hours before it picks a plan.

## Watch out for

Hours multiply by language. One hour in three languages spends three translation hours, so the $99 plan's 25 hours are about eight hours of a trilingual service, not 25 Sundays. Voice cloning starts on Advanced, at $299/month. The Standard yearly savings figure on the page is broken markup, so do not budget from it. New accounts can try 4 hours free.
