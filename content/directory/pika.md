Pika is a studio in front of several video, image, and speech models, plus its own. The free plan lets you try the models with credit packs. It does not include a monthly credit grant. Starter, at $10 a month, is the first plan with 600 credits. The same pricing page documents a Pika MCP, and it says top-up credits can be spent there.

## Best for

Someone who wants one login for a clip, a still, and a voiceover, and who will read the credit quote for the model before generating.

## Watch out for

Free is not a monthly allowance. The comparison table lists its monthly credits as zero, with packs only. Creator and Fancy add a daily Pika H3 Flex allowance that Starter does not have. Fancy is a range: the card starts at $95 a month, and the comparison row goes to $880. Early access to new models starts at $440 a month, on Fancy. A pack bought at 60 credits per dollar cannot be spent on the API, the iOS app, or old.pika.art. The FAQ's Seedance example, about 122 credits for 5 seconds at 720p, is an estimate on Starter, not a flat rate for every model.
