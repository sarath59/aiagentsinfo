Stalkr is Marc Lou's social listening tool for founders. You add product names, competitors, or niche phrases. It pulls public mentions from X, Reddit, YouTube, and LinkedIn, then sorts them into cases such as bugs, complaints, and testimonials. It does not reply for you. A new account starts with 100 free mentions.
