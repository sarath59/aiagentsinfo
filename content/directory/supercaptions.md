Supercaptions is a caption tool built for code-switched Indian speech: Hinglish, Tenglish, and the Indian languages named on the pricing cards. You get word-by-word captions, a timing editor, and a 4K export. The first 2 minutes are free. After that, the smallest purchase is ₹49 once for 15 minutes, and those credits do not expire.

## Best for

A creator posting Reels in Hinglish or another Indian language who wants captions timed to the word and does not want a subscription for a handful of videos.

## Watch out for

The prices are rupees, and the page says payment is Razorpay. A card billed in dollars is a conversion on top of ₹49, ₹249, or ₹449. The monthly plans are minutes per month. The page does not say those minutes roll over. The one-time pack does not expire, and it is the worse rate per minute (about ₹3.27 against about ₹1.50 on Creator). The product is aimed at Indian accents and Indian languages. It does not claim to be a general English caption desk. No Stripe revenue figure is published for it. A TrustMRR profile exists and is verified with Razorpay, so no revenue is quoted here.
