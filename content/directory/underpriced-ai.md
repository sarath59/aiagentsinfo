Underpriced AI is for people who sell secondhand goods. You photograph an item. The app names it, shows what similar listings recently sold for, and drafts the marketplace listing. You still check the draft and press post. eBay and Bonanza publishing is in the product. Other marketplaces can be filled in from the browser extension, and you post those yourself.
