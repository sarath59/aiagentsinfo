Koe, from Hong Xuan Kow, is an AI video studio aimed at a series rather than a one-off clip. The pricing page says every plan includes character limits and a storyboard, and that you can change a clip with a sentence. The model table on that page names LTX 2.3 Pro, Kling 3.0, Kling O3, and Seedance 2.0. Hobby, the first paid plan, is $12/month.

## Best for

A creator who needs the same characters across scenes and wants the credit cost on the plan card before they render.

## Watch out for

The Hobby card says both "commercial rights" and "Koe watermark on exports." The watermark line drops off at Starter, which is $30/month. Yearly billing is advertised at 20% off. The monthly dollar amounts above are the ones printed on the cards. TrustMRR's pricing blurb still says Hobby $10 and Starter $24. That is not what koe.sh/pricing showed on November 11, 2026.

TrustMRR verifies revenue with a Stripe API key. Checked November 11, 2026, when that page was last updated at 4:17 AM: MRR $5,858, last 30 days $5,058, all-time revenue $77,762, with 302 active subscriptions.
