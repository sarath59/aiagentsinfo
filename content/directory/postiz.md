Postiz, from Nevo David, is the scheduler you hand to an agent. The hosted product and the [AGPL-3.0](https://github.com/gitroomhq/postiz-app) repo both exist. Every paid plan includes the MCP server, the CLI, and the API, so Claude, Codex, Cursor, or another [agent](/glossary/agent) can schedule without a separate agent tier. Standard is the $29 plan: 5 channels and unlimited posts.

## Best for

A builder who already runs an agent and wants that agent to file posts across X, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and the other networks the pricing FAQ lists.

## Watch out for

Posts are unlimited. AI video is not. Standard includes 3 AI videos and 60 minutes of clipping a month. One clipping minute is charged per minute of source video, however many clips come out. The pricing FAQ says self-hosting is a secondary option and that agent features ship on the hosted product first. Refunds cover up to one month if the last charge was within 120 days. Annual plans are not refundable.

TrustMRR verifies revenue with a Stripe API key. Checked November 11, 2026, when that page was last updated at 8:24 AM: MRR $402,847, last 30 days $443,718, all-time revenue $1,841,008, with 11,857 active subscriptions. The same page says a significant share of recurring revenue is billed through Whop and is not in those Stripe totals.
