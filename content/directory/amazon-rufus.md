Alexa for Shopping is the name Amazon now uses in its store for the assistant that used to be presented as Rufus. Amazon's [help page](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=Tvh55TTsQ5XQSFc7Pr) says Rufus and Alexa are being brought together, and that the store experience appears as Alexa for Shopping on the Amazon Shopping app and on Amazon.com.

Amazon's [guide updated October 29, 2026](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/how-to-use-amazon-shopping-ai-assistant) says all U.S. customers can use it. The same guide separates a cart you still check out yourself, an auto-buy that purchases when a price you set is reached, and Buy for Me for eligible products on other stores. Amazon's [Buy for Me article](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/amazon-shopping-app-buy-for-me-brands) says the brand sends the confirmation and handles delivery, returns, and customer service. An [October 29 post](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/alexa-for-shopping-new-capabilities) says more than 100 account actions, including lists, registries, Subscribe and Save, and billing questions, are available to all U.S. customers.

## What are the latest Amazon Rufus features and expansion in November 2026?

On November 6, 2026, Amazon published text shopping for Alexa for Shopping. The same post was updated on November 13, 2026 to say the feature is now widely available to all Alexa for Shopping customers.

Amazon’s [About Amazon post](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/alexa-for-shopping-text-to-shop), published November 6, 2026 and updated November 13, says customers can text a contact named “Alexa Shopping” to search, compare, add items, and complete a purchase. A reorder example is bought with the customer’s approval. The update line says the November 13 change is that texting is widely available to all Alexa for Shopping customers. The page still points customers at a landing-page sign-up. The post says a request started over text can carry over to the Amazon Shopping app, Amazon.com, and Alexa-enabled devices. It also names Shop Direct for products from stores across the web, photo search, price-drop alerts, and up to a year of price history. Amazon says more than 400 million customers used Alexa for Shopping in the last 12 months, and that use of its agentic features grew more than eight times since the start of the year. Those figures are Amazon’s.
