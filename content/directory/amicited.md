AmICited, from FlowHunt and founder Yasha, asks the answer engines a prompt you choose and records whether your domain is cited. The same credits pay for those answers, for agent runs, and for AI articles. The pricing page also lists an MCP server for Claude Code and Codex, so an agent can pull the citation data instead of you exporting a sheet.

## Best for

A brand or agency that already knows its prompts and wants a credit bill tied to how often those prompts are checked.

## Watch out for

Starter does not include Copilot, Claude, Grok, or DeepSeek. Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, and GA4 start on Pro. Credits reset. A quiet month does not bank them. The trial needs a card. Prices exclude VAT, so a EU invoice is higher than the card price. The comparison table's MCP checks are easier to misread than the euro prices. Confirm the box for your tier before you assume Starter includes the server.
