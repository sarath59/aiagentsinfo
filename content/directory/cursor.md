## What is Cursor AI?

Cursor AI is Anysphere’s code editor with an agent that can edit a repository. New customers buy it inside The X Subscription, one subscription for Grok, Cursor, and X, rather than as a separate Cursor-only plan.

The [pricing docs](https://cursor.com/docs/models-and-pricing), read November 9, 2026, say paid plans include a weekly pool of usage for Cursor’s own models, shared with Grok. Those models are Grok 4.7, Grok 4.6, Grok 4.5, and Composer 2.5. Plus runs them at fixed settings and does not include third-party models, Auto, Cloud Agents, Automations, or Bugbot. Premium and above add those, and third-party models are billed at API list price from prepaid credit. People who subscribed before The X Subscription stay on a legacy Cursor plan until they switch or cancel.

## How much does Cursor cost?

On November 9, 2026, [cursor.com/pricing](https://cursor.com/pricing) lists Free at $0, Plus at $8 a month, Premium at $30, Super at $100, and Ultra at $200. Annual billing is $80, $300, $1,000, and $2,000. Teams is $30, $100, or $200 per seat a month. Enterprise is custom. Prices exclude tax, and third-party model use is not included in the monthly price.

Plus is the plan that includes only Grok models. Premium is 4 times Plus usage, Super is 15 times, and Ultra is 50 times, on the docs page read the same day. The pool refreshes weekly.
