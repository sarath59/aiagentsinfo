Vivify is a prompt workspace for stills and short clips, on one credit balance. You see the credit cost before a generation starts. Basic on the quarterly card is $10.56 a month, billed $31.68 every three months, for 500 credits. The monthly Basic price in the same page's plan data is $12. The site is operated by Nano Agent LLC. TrustMRR names the founder as Chris Y.

## Best for

A creator who wants images, edits, and a short video from the same credit balance, and who will check the quote before hitting generate.

## Watch out for

Quarterly is the price on the open tab. Monthly is higher: $12, $29, and $89 for the same credit counts. Max is a slider. The $62.30 quarterly figure is the 8,500-credit step, not the top of the control. Video and images share the balance, so a few clips can spend a month of stills. The page says one-time pack credits do not expire, and the paid-plan feature list says unused subscription credits carry to the next month. Those are different rules. The homepage says you can start with free credits and does not print how many.
