HeyGen is a video studio built around a presenter. Free lets you try Avatar IV and Video Agent on three short, watermarked clips. Creator, at $29 a month, is the plan that removes the branding and adds a custom avatar with your own voice. Pro is where the page puts 4K, longer videos, and named third-party models.

## Best for

A marketer who needs a person on camera, in more than one language, without booking a shoot.

## Watch out for

Free stops at 1 minute and keeps the watermark. Creator's voice allowance is 10 hours of text to speech. Pro raises that to 16-plus hours and names Seedance 2.5 and Veo 3.1, so those models are a Pro feature, not a free one. The FAQ states the Creator annual rate as $24 a month. The page advertises 2 months free on yearly billing and does not print a separate annual dollar amount for Pro or Business on the cards read November 11. Credits on the paid individual plans roll over one month, not forever.
