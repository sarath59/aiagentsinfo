GETebook.ai is the page behind searches for getebook. Bogdan Dragomir's product writes a short book from a topic, an audience, and a tone: an outline, chapters, and a cover, then a file. It is built for a coach or a marketer who needs a lead magnet or a paid download. It is not a line editor for a novelist, even though a novel is one of the credit rows.

## Pricing

getebook.ai/pricing was fetched twice on 16 Nov 2026. Both responses were HTTP 200 and 50,736 bytes, the same file. There is one product, Pro, and six credit sizes. Every size includes the same feature set. The page says you can change size at any time, and that an upgrade is prorated. Billing is monthly. There is no yearly price on the page.

| Monthly price | Credits that month |
| --- | --- |
| $29 | 300 |
| $39 | 500 |
| $49 | 750 |
| $59 | 1,000 |
| $79 | 1,500 |
| $99 | 2,000 |

The $29 card is marked most popular. The features listed on it, and stated as included on every size, are a monthly credit refresh, unlimited PDF export, full commercial rights, priority support, and an analytics dashboard. Preview is free. PDF, EPUB, and DOCX download requires Pro. The page says you can export the moment you subscribe. Cancel anytime, and access lasts until the end of the period you already paid.

Credits reset each billing cycle. They do not roll over. Running out means you upgrade, which is prorated, or you wait for the refresh.

## What a credit buys

The pricing page prints the cost, the chapter range, and the cost to regenerate.

| Type | Credits | Chapters | Regen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead magnet | 25 | 5 to 7 | 13 |
| Research report | 30 | 8 to 12 | 15 |
| How-to guide | 35 | 10 to 12 | 18 |
| Interactive workbook | 35 | 10 to 15 | 18 |
| Online course | 45 | 15 to 20 | 23 |
| Fiction novel | 60 | 20 to 30 | 30 |

On the $29 plan, 300 credits is 12 lead magnets if you never regenerate, or 10 if each one needs a regen at 13 credits (25 + 13 = 38, and 300 / 38 is about 7 full cycles, not 12). Do the sum for the type you actually ship. A novel is 60 credits, so the entry plan is five novels with nothing left over, or four novels if each one is regenerated at 30 credits (90 credits, and 300 / 90 is three with 30 left). A how-to at 35, regenerated once at 18, is 53 credits, and 300 / 53 is five guides with change. The chapter counts are the page's ranges, not a promise that a 7-chapter lead magnet is longer than a 5-chapter one in words. The page does not print a word count.

Commercial rights are on the plan, in the site's words "full commercial rights." You still have to read the draft. The tool produces the outline, the chapters, and a cover. It does not claim a human editor. Analytics on the dashboard is listed. The page does not say which numbers that dashboard shows.

## Who it is for

Someone who will spend the month on lead magnets, short reports, and guides, and who can edit before the file goes out. A $29 month of lead magnets is a real quantity, about a dozen if you accept the first draft. A $29 month of novels is a few drafts, not a catalog. The higher tiers are the same product with a larger pile of credits, not a different editor. Pick the pile. At $59 you have 1,000 credits, which is 40 lead magnets at 25, or 16 novels at 60. At $99 you have 2,000, which is 80 lead magnets or 33 novels, again before regens.

## API and agents

The pricing page and the pages fetched for this check do not document a public API, an MCP server, or a way for an agent to spend the credits. Generation happens in the product. If you need a book pipeline inside another agent, this is not the integration. [StoryShort](/directory/storyshort) is the video product on this site that does document an API and an MCP server. GETebook does not, on the pages read today.

## Best for

A coach, a consultant, or a marketer who already knows the topic and the reader, wants an outline and a cover in one place, and will rewrite anything that should not go out under their name. The free preview is the way to see a chapter before paying.

## Watch out for

Credits die at the end of the month. A regen is not free, and it is not the full price either. It is the third column. People who iterate will spend the regen column every time, and the "$29 is 12 lead magnets" line will be wrong for them. Preview does not include the download. There is no yearly discount on the page, so the monthly price is the price. Unlimited PDF export means the export action is not a second meter. The credits still limit how many books exist to export. No Stripe-verified revenue figure was re-read for this update, so the older TrustMRR numbers are not repeated as current. The commercial-rights line is the site's. It is not a copyright opinion. A novel of 20 to 30 chapters from a topic prompt is a draft with that structure, not a book a publisher has accepted.

## Alternatives

[ColorCraft](/directory/colorcraft) builds coloring books, storybooks, comics, and puzzle books for KDP, with a cover wrap, from $9 a month. It draws pages. GETebook writes prose chapters. [Koala](/directory/koala) is the other writing tool in the directory for long content, if you want articles rather than a bound file. [MagicSlides](/directory/magicslides) exports a deck to PDF, PowerPoint, or Google Slides, which is a different file. [ReplyCurrent](/directory/replycurrent) answers questions from PDFs you already have. It does not write the PDF. None of them uses this six-row credit table.
