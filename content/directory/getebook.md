GETebook.ai, from Bogdan Dragomir, writes a short book from a topic, an audience, and a tone. It produces the outline, the chapters, and a cover, then exports PDF, EPUB, or Word. It is built for a coach or marketer who needs a lead magnet or a paid download, not for a novelist who wants line edits.

## Best for

Someone who will spend credits on lead magnets, guides, and short reports, and who can edit the draft before it goes out.

## Watch out for

Credits die at the end of the month. A $29 plan holds 300 credits. A lead magnet is 25 credits and a novel is 60, so the entry plan is about twelve lead magnets or five novels, not a blank check. You can preview without paying. You cannot download the file until you subscribe. There is no yearly price on the page.
