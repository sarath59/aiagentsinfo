Frutti makes a short vertical episode from a one-line idea: script, a cast that carries across episodes, voices, burned-in captions, music, and a 9:16 MP4. The engine on the pricing card is Frutti E1. Starter is $18 a month for 1,200 credits, and the same card says a Standard episode is 1,200 credits. One episode is the month.

## Best for

A faceless channel that wants the same characters back next episode, and that can live with about one Standard episode on the cheapest paid plan.

## Watch out for

The free step stops at the script. There is no free render and no watermarked trial render. Creator, at 2,500 credits, is about two Standard episodes, or one Long episode (1,800) plus a clip. The yearly Starter price is $7 a month, billed for the year up front, not $7 you can cancel in month two. The Starter card says annual billing is 61% off, and the FAQ says yearly billing is up to 70% off. Both sentences are on the page. TrustMRR verifies revenue with a Stripe API key.
