## ElevenAgents Campaign Manager

On November 9, 2026, ElevenLabs published Campaign Manager for ElevenAgents ([announcement](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/elevenagents-campaign-manager)). It takes a contact list and an outcome, then retries, calls back, and redirects inside calling windows you set. The [docs](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-agents/phone-numbers/campaigns), read November 10, set a default cap of 6 attempts per recipient and a maximum of 30, reject zero-retention workspaces, and list phone voice, WhatsApp voice, and WhatsApp text.
