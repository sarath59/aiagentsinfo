Crayo is a clipping editor. AutoClip finds the moments, frames them, and adds captions. You can also take the moments and cut them yourself. The homepage schema names the company Crayo, Inc., founded January 2, 2024, and names co-founders Aleem Rehmtulla, Musa Mustafa, and Daniel Bitton. Hobby is the $29 monthly plan, or $19 a month when $57 is billed every three months.

## Best for

Someone who already records long video and wants the shorts, the captions, and a cap they can see before export.

## Watch out for

Workflow credits and export hours are separate meters. Finding a moment spends a credit. Exporting the finished file spends hours. Hobby stops at 2 export hours and 30 voiceover minutes, even if credits remain. Plan credits refill every month and do not roll over, on monthly, quarterly, and yearly terms. The pricing FAQ says there are no refunds. Cancel keeps the plan until the period ends. The yearly toggle advertises a save of up to 48% without printing the yearly total on the cards read November 12.
