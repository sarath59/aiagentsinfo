## What are Perplexity Teammates?

Perplexity’s [help center article](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/2026102601-perplexity-teammates), read November 7, 2026, says Teammates are AI agents that own ongoing work. You give a teammate a responsibility, such as triaging bug reports, tracking metrics, or researching a market, and it plans the tasks and follows through until the result is ready for review. Each teammate has its own role. It can monitor tools you connect, act when something changes, and reach out when it needs your input. The same article says the initial research preview requires an individual Perplexity Max subscription and is on the web. Enterprise Pro and Enterprise Max are not included by default.

The [product page](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/products/teammate) says Teammates are available for Max subscribers on the web, with iOS and Android still listed as coming, and that a teammate works in the background and notifies you when a decision is needed.
