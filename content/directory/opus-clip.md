Opus Clip takes a long video and cuts the shorts. Starter, at $15 a month, is the individual plan: 150 credits, captions, a virality score, and a path out to YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, or a file. The trial does not ask for a card.

## Best for

A creator who already has the long recording and wants the clips, the captions, and the watermark gone without a full editor.

## Watch out for

Starter cannot be bought yearly. The Pro control on the page also steps through credit packs, so the price on the button changes. The annual figure read on November 11, 2026 was $174 a year, shown as $14.50 a month, with 3,600 credits spread over those 12 months. Unused credits roll for 2 months, not indefinitely. Business, the API-heavy plan, is a quote.
