Cartesia is the speech layer and the phone agent in one bill. Sonic is text to speech. Ink is speech to text. Managed agents place and answer calls. The agent rate on the pricing page is $0.03 a minute, printed next to a previous $0.06. Free includes $1 of that time, about 33 minutes at the published rate. Pro, at $5 a month, is the first paid plan.

## Best for

A developer who wants the voice model and the calling agent from the same vendor, with a credit balance for speech and a separate dollar balance for agent minutes.

## Watch out for

Every paid plan is two allowances, not one. Credits pay for TTS and STT. The prepaid agent dollars pay for call minutes at $0.03. When the agent dollars run out, the credits do not automatically become minutes. A Cartesia phone number is another $0.014 a minute on top of the agent rate. The $0.03 figure is marked down from $0.06 on the same page, so the rate has already moved once. Enterprise is a quote. The free $1 is a small test, not a production line.
