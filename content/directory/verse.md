Verse sells one company run by an AI team, not a chat box with a job title. On Solo, at $99 a month, the card includes a site and store, launch and social posts, tools the team builds, browser and computer use, and 1,000+ connectors. The comparison table says Verse takes 0% of sales on Free, Solo, Professional, and Scale. Free is real, and it is small: 50 credits, once, for one company.

## Best for

A small-business owner who wants an [AI employee](/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee) team for the site, the social posts, and the busywork, and who will still approve what the team does.

## Watch out for

The free 50 credits do not renew. Solo's 3,500 credits are the monthly pool for the whole company, including browser and computer use, which spends credits whenever the team is driving a machine. The page shows an Annual control and a Monthly control. The dollars that rendered are the monthly figures above. This check did not read a second set of annual prices, so do not assume $99 is a yearly rate. No Stripe-verified revenue page was found for Verse on November 13, 2026, so no revenue is quoted.
