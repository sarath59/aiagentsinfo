Bazzly, searched as bazzly, posts and replies on Reddit for a product using warmed accounts and a credit plan. The pricing page, fetched twice on November 14, 2026, still has one plan. TrustMRR's founder field that day reads Filip Panoski. The pricing page itself points questions to support@bazzly.ai and does not retell a founder story.

The $199 plan includes opportunities, leads, Reply Autopilot, warmed-up accounts, smart upvotes, the browser extension, the public API, and the MCP server, plus priority support. You start at bazzly.ai/dashboard. A second active project is another seat at the same $199, prorated for the rest of the period.

## What a credit buys

The pricing page prints a cost table. A post through a warmed-up account is 50 credits. A comment through a warmed-up account is 10 credits. An upvote is 0.2 credits. An AI draft of a reply or a DM is 0.1 credits. Autopilot AI drafts are free. A comment posted from your own account through the browser extension is free. Sending a DM from your own account through the extension is free. Finding opportunities, leads, and brand signals is free. The page's own example: a warmed-account comment with 5 upvotes costs 11 credits. On Autopilot at the default Moderate pace, two comments a day with upvotes come to roughly 22 credits a day. At that pace, 170 credits is about a week of that pattern, not a month of heavy posting. Do the division for your own pace. The page gives the rates so you can.

Top-ups are $1 per credit. Presets are 25, 50, 100, and 200 credits, or any amount from $5 to $1,000. The minimum is the $5 floor, not a $1 checkout.

## The trial

Seven days, no charge on the day you start. A payment method is added at checkout. The first charge happens when the trial ends, and the page says you get a reminder three days before. You get 5 trial credits, and they are only for AI drafts of replies and DMs. During the trial you can see opportunities and leads, draft replies, post from your own account with the extension, and send DMs. Posting through warmed-up accounts, upvotes, managed posts, and Autopilot need the plan to be active. Trial credits cannot be spent on those. The API and MCP work during the trial. Cancel before the trial ends and nothing is charged. You can activate early from the dashboard, which starts the plan, unlocks warmed-up posting, and grants the full 170 credits immediately.

## Best for

A founder who wants Reddit replies in threads that already match the product, and who will watch the credit table instead of assuming 170 covers a month of warmed posts.

## Watch out for

One warmed post is 50 credits, so the 170-credit month is three of those posts with a little left, or a larger number of cheaper comments. The trial looks like the product and then withholds the warmed accounts and Autopilot until you pay. The card is collected at the start. Credits do not become a second project. A second project is another $199. MCP and the API are included, which matters if an agent is posting for you, and they are the same credit pool. Reddit can still remove a post. Bazzly's credit is not a refund for that.

## Alternatives

[traxy](/directory/traxy) looks for leads on LinkedIn and starts at $149 a month for up to 500 leads. [GojiberryAI](/directory/gojiberryai) and [MimikFlow](/directory/mimikflow) are the other sales listings. [Taplio](/directory/taplio) and [SuperX](/directory/superx) are for publishing on LinkedIn or X, not for warmed Reddit accounts. None of those is a substitute credit system. Compare the action you are buying: a Reddit comment, a LinkedIn lead, or a post on your own profile.
