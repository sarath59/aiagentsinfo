AiVids Pro is a faceless YouTube studio. One prompt becomes a script, a voiceover, visuals, and a rendered short or a long video. If you connect a channel, it can publish through the YouTube API. Starter is $19 a month and 1,000 credits. The homepage shows an estimate before the render, down to the image count and the clip seconds.

## Best for

A creator who wants a faceless channel and wants the credit cost of a specific look on the screen before the job starts.

## Watch out for

The "about 10 shorts" line is the Illustrated look, not a footage video. Seedance 2 Mini at 480p is 9.5 credits a second, so a 20-second clip is 190 credits before the images and the script. The 200 free credits cover a first short on the page's example, not a month of posting. There is no separate pricing URL.
