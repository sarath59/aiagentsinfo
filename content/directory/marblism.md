Marblism sells one pool of hours and seven named AI employees, not seven products. Eva handles email, Sonny posts to social, Stan searches and writes to leads, Penny writes SEO, Rachel answers the phone, Walter edits the website, and Linda drafts legal documents. Ulric Musset is named co-founder and CEO on the [about page](https://www.marblism.com/about-us). The [Y Combinator listing](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/marblism) puts the company in Winter 2024. The yearly base card on November 12, 2026 was $24 a month.

## Best for

A small business that wants inbox, social, leads, SEO, calls, a site, and contracts on one bill, with a person still approving the work.

## Watch out for

An hour here is a tariff, not a clock hour. The help article charges 5 minutes to draft an email, 30 minutes for a social post, 1 hour for a blog post, 10 minutes for a call, and 16 minutes per second of SD video (36 minutes per second in HD). A 10-second SD video is about 2.7 hours. Chat that asks for work spends the same hours. Configuring agents and approving work do not. Unused hours do not roll over.

The pricing-page FAQ says you can buy flexible top-ups when hours run out. The help article says there is no one-off top-up and that more hours means a bigger plan. Read both before you buy. On the same help page, the video-cap table allows 50 hours of video on the 100-hour plan, and a later sentence says 30. The first site build and the first two videos are free. The comparison prices printed for other products on the pricing page are dated October 7, 2026, and they are Marblism's own index.
