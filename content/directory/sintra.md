Sintra is a set of named AI employees in one workspace, not twelve products. The roster on the site includes Soshie for social, Penn for copy, Seomi for SEO, Cassie for support, Milli for sales, and Vizzy as the assistant. You onboard the business once. The workers are chats that share that context. The yearly card on November 11, 2026 showed $15.60 a month.

## Best for

A solo owner who wants a role for the task, a shared brand memory, and a person still approving the result.

## Watch out for

The prices on the cards are the discounted figures, printed next to higher numbers and a "save" label. There is no free trial. Refunds are a 14-day window. Every card includes 250 credits a month. The FAQ says a 15-second video is about 40 credits, so video spends the month quickly. The same FAQ describes a website as 15 to 40 credits and, later, 30 credits for a simple build and 300-plus for a store. The page says Sintra is the wrong tool if you want a coding agent, enterprise controls, or posts that publish with no approval.
