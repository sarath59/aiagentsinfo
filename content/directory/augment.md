Augment Code sells the coding agent as one bill for the team. Standard is $20 a month for as many as 50 seats, with $20 of model and compute usage in the price. People who barely open it do not add a license fee. Heavy users draw from the same pool.

## Best for

A team that wants a shared usage balance, a CLI, and [MCP](/glossary/model-context-protocol), and that does not want a seat line for every developer.

## Watch out for

The included $20 or $100 is dollars, not a request quota. Past it, LLM calls cost the provider's public API price plus a 40% service fee. Compute is extra without that fee. Top-ups that are not part of the plan expire 12 months after you buy them. Standard and Business both stop at 50 seats. Enterprise is the plan that drops the seat cap and adds SSO, SCIM, and CMEK. Paid plans say your data is not used for training.
