SocialClaw, from Nardi Braho, is the posting layer you hand to an agent. You connect the accounts once. The agent then uploads media, checks the schedule, and publishes through the MCP server, the CLI, or the workspace API. The people it fits are builders who already run Claude, Codex, or another agent and do not want that agent holding each network's login.

## Best for

A solo operator who wants one workspace key between an agent and the social accounts, with a hard cap on how many posts go out.

## Watch out for

Every paid plan meters posts, not just accounts. Starter stops at 200 posts a month. The nav says the scheduler covers 11 networks, while the same page lists 12 names. Yearly billing is advertised at 20% off, but the Starter markup also carries a $10-a-month yearly figure. Read the toggle for the plan you will buy before you budget a year.

TrustMRR verifies revenue with a Lemon Squeezy API key. Checked November 10, 2026: current MRR $1,804, all-time revenue $7,367, with 92 active subscriptions. TrustMRR last updated that page at 4:25 AM the same day.
