Clueso turns a recording of your product into two things: a video and a step-by-step article. [Y Combinator's company page](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/clueso) lists it in the Winter 2023 batch. You can start from a screen recording, a slide deck, or a PDF. The comparison table marks Clueso MCP as included on Free as well as the paid plans, so an agent can sit on the same account.

## Best for

A product or support team that needs the help article and the video from one capture, and that will pay yearly.

## Watch out for

The prices above are the yearly billing view. Solo at $40 a month is labeled "billed yearly." This read of the page did not show the month-to-month rate, so do not quote $40 as that rate. The Solo card says 3 hours of video and 180 articles a year. The comparison table says 15 minutes and 15 articles a month. Those match. They are not two different quotas. AI credits are used for chat creation, chat editing, avatars, motion graphics, and image generation. The FAQ says they are not used for voiceover, script rewriting, or the step-by-step guides.
