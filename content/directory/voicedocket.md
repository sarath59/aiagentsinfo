VoiceDocket is a Mac app that turns an English recording into a paginated PDF with timestamps. It is a transcript, not a phone agent and not a captioned video. You preview the whole document free. The preview pages are watermarked. Paying $29.99 once is what removes that and lets you export. Speaker labels are grouped on macOS 15 and later, and the page says you review and correct them.

## Best for

A lawyer or a consultant on a Mac who has an English recording and wants a PDF with line-level timestamps, paid once.

## Watch out for

English only. Apple silicon only. macOS 14 is the floor, and speaker groups start at macOS 15. The speech models are a 511 MB download before the first transcript, so the first run is not instant on a slow connection. The watermark stays until you buy the license. There is no monthly plan and no Windows or iPhone app on this page. Revenue is not quoted. The TrustMRR profile for this app is connected to the App Store, not to Stripe.
