traxy, searched under that spelling, finds people already talking in a market, attaches a verified work email, and on higher plans drafts outbound from that research. The pricing page describes it as high-intent leads on LinkedIn who fit an ideal customer profile, with LinkedIn and email outreach. TrustMRR's founder field, checked November 14, 2026, reads Ben Buaron. The product site's pricing markdown does not repeat a founder bio, so the name stays attributed to TrustMRR.

The agent on every paid plan is called trax. The pricing page says trax is one chat to find and review leads and to change your setup, and that discovery runs find qualified leads so you are not searching by hand. Leads arrive with a work email that has been verified. You can track as many profiles as you want and get a notice when one shows a buying signal. Leads can be sent to Slack, Meta Ads, and what the page calls 8+ more tools.

[traxy.ai/pricing.md](https://traxy.ai/pricing.md) was fetched twice on November 14, 2026. The two files matched. The HTML pricing page's meta description matches the same monthly numbers and the 20% yearly line. The markdown is the table.

Yearly billing saves 20%. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial. Signup is app.traxy.ai/signup.

Pro is described for a solo founder. It includes trax, discovery runs, verified email, unlimited tracking, and the Slack and Meta Ads routing. Advanced adds campaigns: research each lead and prepare LinkedIn and email sequences that you test and approve before launch. Phone numbers are an add-on, not in the base Advanced price. Scale adds a dedicated account strategist, a dedicated Slack channel, and done-for-you outbound as a further add-on, on top of phone numbers. Agencies add multi-client workspaces, partner pricing, and shared reporting. The markdown points agency buyers to a Calendly link and everyone else to a 20-minute demo.

The comparison table in that markdown marks trax, verified email, unlimited tracking, and the integrations as yes on all four columns. Campaigns start at Advanced. The lead caps are the row that changes the bill.

## Best for

A sales team that wants leads from people already active in the market, with a work email attached, and that will still approve sequences before they send.

## Watch out for

The meter is new qualified leads per month, not emails sent. Five hundred on Pro is the cap on what traxy surfaces, not a promise that 500 will reply. Campaigns, the part that writes the sequence, start at Advanced, $399 a month. Phone numbers and done-for-you outbound are add-ons even on Scale. Yearly is the lower per-month number ($119, $319, $879) because you are billed on the yearly term. The 7-day trial is on every plan. Read what happens to the card at the end of the trial in the signup flow. This page does not print a "no card" line. Agencies are custom. Do not quote a fifth price.

## Alternatives

[GojiberryAI](/directory/gojiberryai) and [MimikFlow](/directory/mimikflow) are the other sales listings. [Bazzly](/directory/bazzly) finds customers on Reddit, not LinkedIn, and bills one $199 plan. [Taplio](/directory/taplio) and [Tweet Hunter](/directory/tweet-hunter) help you post and reply on LinkedIn or X. They are not a lead database with a verified email. Use traxy when the output you want is a person and a work email, and one of those when the output is a post.
