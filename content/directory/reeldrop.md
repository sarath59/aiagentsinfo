ReelDrop, from Piyush Sachdeva, is an Instagram scheduler with the automation tied to the post before it goes live. A comment on a Reel can open a DM thread the moment the post publishes. The agent path is an MCP server on every plan, including the free one, so Claude or another client can schedule and read the inbox inside that plan's limits.

## Best for

A creator with one Instagram account who wants comment-to-DM flows and is fine starting on the free plan.

## Watch out for

Free DMs carry the line "Sent via reeldrop.io". Free and Influencer both stop at 3 AI caption generations a month. AI carousel images and reel thumbnails start on Creator. The plans count Instagram accounts only. Prices in India are ₹449 and ₹2,699, not the US dollar cards. Creator's 1,000 AI DM replies reset on a 30-day window.
