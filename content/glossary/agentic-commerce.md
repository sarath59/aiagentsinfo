## What is agentic commerce?

Agentic commerce is shopping in which an agent can discover, compare, and sometimes pay, with a person’s permission. The store remains the seller.

Google’s Canada post on November 4, 2026 uses the phrase for AI that completes a task on a person’s behalf, with that person’s permission. Visa’s April 30, 2025 announcement uses it for agents that find and buy inside limits the cardholder set. The payment step is [agentic checkout](/glossary/agentic-checkout). The software that takes the step is a [shopping agent](/glossary/shopping-agent).

The credential is the part people skip. A [product feed](/glossary/product-feed) lets the agent quote a price. A [delegated payment token](/glossary/delegated-payment-token), a network [payment token](/glossary/payment-token), an AP2 mandate, a Visa purchase instruction, or a Mastercard agentic token is what lets someone charge. [Know Your Agent](/glossary/know-your-agent) is Mastercard’s registration rule for that last token. Visa’s parallel control is its Agent Registry and Trusted Agent Protocol. They are separate programs. ChatGPT Wallet, opened November 13, 2026, is the network-token path inside a dot: [How does ChatGPT Wallet work?](/blog/how-does-chatgpt-wallet-work).
