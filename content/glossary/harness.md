> **Sample glossary term.**

A **harness** is not the model weights. It is the software that feeds the goal, exposes [tools](/glossary/tool-use), stores observations, retries, and stops. Two products can share a model and behave differently because the harness differs.

See [How agent harnesses work](/blog/how-agent-harnesses-work) and the [harness comparison](/trackers/harnesses) stub.
