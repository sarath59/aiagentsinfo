**Autonomy** here means permission and supervision, not consciousness. A suggest-only coding assistant is low autonomy. A [shopping agent](/glossary/shopping-agent) that can complete an [agentic checkout](/glossary/agentic-checkout) is higher. The [harness](/glossary/harness) encodes the level: budgets, allowlists, and human gates. The shopping guide is [Can an AI shopping agent check out for you?](/blog/can-an-ai-shopping-agent-check-out-for-you).
