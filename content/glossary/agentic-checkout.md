**Agentic checkout** is the payment step inside a [shopping agent](/glossary/shopping-agent). The assistant sends the cart and a payment instruction. The merchant charges the payment method on the merchant's systems and remains the seller of record, on the Google, OpenAI, Microsoft, and Perplexity pages cited in the [checkout guide](/blog/can-an-ai-shopping-agent-check-out-for-you). The sourced table is [Best AI agent for shopping](/best/ai-agents-for-shopping).

The step is a [tool](/glossary/tool-use) call with a [guardrail](/glossary/guardrail). The guardrail can be a tap, a price cap, or a rule set earlier, such as Amazon's auto-buy at a target price. How much of that happens without a fresh confirmation is [autonomy](/glossary/autonomy). The catalog the assistant is allowed to quote is a [product feed](/glossary/product-feed). The surrounding software is the [harness](/glossary/harness).
