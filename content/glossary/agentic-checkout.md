## What is agentic checkout?

Agentic checkout is a purchase an assistant can finish. The store remains the seller, and a person or an earlier rule approves the payment.

It is the payment step inside a [shopping agent](/glossary/shopping-agent). The assistant sends the cart and a payment instruction. The merchant charges the payment method on the merchant's systems and remains the seller of record, on the Google, OpenAI, Microsoft, and Perplexity pages cited in the [checkout guide](/blog/can-an-ai-shopping-agent-check-out-for-you). Amazon’s November 6, 2026 text feature is on [Amazon Rufus latest features](/directory/amazon-rufus). The six-company timeline is [AI shopping agents next 6 months](/reports/ai-shopping-agents-next-6-months). The spec versions behind UCP and ACP, read November 10, 2026, are [OpenAI’s checkout example is still API version 2025-09-12](/blog/ucp-vs-acp-spec-versions-november-2026). The sourced table is [Best AI agent for shopping](/best/ai-agents-for-shopping). Shopping coverage is collected on [Agents in shopping](/topics/shopping).

The step is a [tool](/glossary/tool-use) call with a [guardrail](/glossary/guardrail). The guardrail can be a tap, a price cap, or a rule set earlier, such as Amazon's auto-buy at a target price. How much of that happens without a fresh confirmation is [autonomy](/glossary/autonomy). The catalog the assistant is allowed to quote is a [product feed](/glossary/product-feed). The surrounding software is the [harness](/glossary/harness).
