**Human on the loop** means a person supervises a run and can step in. The action proceeds under the rules already set. The person watches telemetry, traces, or another record, and intervenes when that record shows a problem.

[Deloitte’s November 18, 2025 essay](https://www.deloitte.com/global/en/insights/industry/technology/technology-media-and-telecom-predictions/2026/ai-agent-orchestration.html), read November 8, 2026, names a spectrum: humans in the loop, on the loop, and out of the loop. It says the first two rely on dashboards for outcome tracing and orchestration so a person can intervene, and it predicts that in 2026 the most advanced organizations will begin shifting some supervision toward human-on-the-loop. That is Deloitte’s forecast.

On this site the phrase stays distinct from [human in the loop](/glossary/human-in-the-loop), which means a person approves, edits, or stops a step before that step counts. How many steps are allowed before someone is asked is [autonomy](/glossary/autonomy). The long explainer is [How do AI agents work?](/blog/how-do-ai-agents-work).
