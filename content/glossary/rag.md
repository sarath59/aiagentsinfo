> **Sample glossary term.**

**Retrieval-augmented generation** looks up text (docs, tickets, prior traces) and adds it to the [context window](/glossary/context-window) before the model answers. It is a memory tactic, not a mind. Agents often mix RAG with tool use; they are not the same thing.
