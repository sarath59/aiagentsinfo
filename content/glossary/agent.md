In classical AI textbooks, an **agent** perceives and acts. On this site the word usually means a **language-model system plus a [harness](/glossary/harness)**: it can call [tools](/glossary/tool-use), look at the result, and continue until a goal or a limit is reached.

A chatbot that only replies in a thread is a useful model. It is not, by itself, an agent in this sense.
