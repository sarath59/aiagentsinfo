A **digital worker**, sometimes called an AI employee, is a label for software that a workplace treats as the holder of a recurring job. On the pages checked for this site’s workplace guide, the software is usually an [agent](/glossary/agent): a model, tools, and a [harness](/glossary/harness) that can plan steps and hand work back. It is a product setting and a management metaphor. It is not a legal employee, a payroll record, or the party accountable for a mistake.

[OpenAI’s business guide](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-business-leaders-guide-to-working-with-agents.pdf), read November 7, 2026, uses “employee agents” for pre-built software that helps a team finish work, and names ChatGPT’s agent mode as the example in that guide. [Deloitte’s December 10, 2025 Tech Trends essay](https://www.deloitte.com/global/en/insights/topics/technology-management/tech-trends/2026/agentic-ai-strategy.html) talks about managing “digital workers” and a “silicon-based workforce” beside human staff, and it keeps people on oversight and on work whose requirements keep shifting. How far the software may go before a person confirms a step is [autonomy](/glossary/autonomy). The stop itself is a [human in the loop](/glossary/human-in-the-loop), often at an [approval gate](/glossary/approval-gate).
