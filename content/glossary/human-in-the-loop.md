**Human in the loop** means a person stays in the run. The [agent](/glossary/agent) can draft, look things up, and propose a next step. A person approves, edits, or stops the step that would send, pay, delete, or otherwise leave the organization.

[OpenAI’s business guide](https://cdn.openai.com/business-guides-and-resources/a-business-leaders-guide-to-working-with-agents.pdf), read November 7, 2026, says guardrails should seek confirmation before many real-world actions and ask for oversight on sensitive tasks such as sending email. [ChatGPT’s Work guide](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/get-started-with-work), read the same day, tells you to say when the task should stop for review, and to follow the notice if a task pauses for a safety check. A [guardrail](/glossary/guardrail) is the check inside the [harness](/glossary/harness). An [approval gate](/glossary/approval-gate) is one configured stop, such as “ask before this write.” [Autonomy](/glossary/autonomy) is how many steps are allowed before that person is asked.

The workplace guide is [What is an AI employee?](/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee). The click-by-click case is [Can an AI agent use your computer?](/blog/can-an-ai-agent-use-your-computer).
