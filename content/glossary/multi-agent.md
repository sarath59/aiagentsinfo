**Multi-agent** setups split work across roles: a planner, a coder, a reviewer. Sometimes that is several model calls with different prompts; sometimes it is several processes. It is still one product, one [harness](/glossary/harness), and one bill. Extra roles are not automatically extra intelligence. How the patterns differ is [How do AI agents work?](/blog/how-do-ai-agents-work). When the other party is an independent agent, the open protocol is [Agent2Agent](/glossary/agent-to-agent-protocol).
