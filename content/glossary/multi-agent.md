---
term: Multi-agent
shortDefinition: More than one model (or role) collaborating, often as planner, worker, or critic.
related:

---

**Multi-agent** setups split work across roles: a planner, a coder, a reviewer. Sometimes that is several model calls with different prompts; sometimes it is several processes. It is still one product, one [harness](/glossary/harness), and one bill. Extra roles are not automatically extra intelligence.
