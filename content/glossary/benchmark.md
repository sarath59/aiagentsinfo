A **benchmark** is an evaluation suite. For agents, the [harness](/glossary/harness) is part of what you are measuring. Read dates, cost, and contamination notes before you believe a chart.

A [behavioral evaluation](/glossary/behavioral-evaluation) checks one action, such as a tool call, rather than the suite’s overall score. The comparison is [How do you evaluate an AI agent harness?](/blog/how-do-you-evaluate-an-ai-agent-harness). How to build the suite is [How to build an eval harness for AI agents](/blog/how-to-build-an-eval-harness-for-ai-agents). Tools that run those checks are [Best AI agent evaluation tools](/best/ai-agent-evaluation-tools). Dated public boards are the [November 2026 harness benchmark](/reports/agent-harness-benchmark-november-2026).
